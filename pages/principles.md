# Principles of Trustworthy AI and ML Models

Defining principles of trustworthy AI and ML models should help us define the core tenets to ensure that these technologies are reliable, fair, and beneficial to society. The following key insights from various research papers outline the foundational principles and considerations for developing trustworthy AI and ML models.

**Trustworthy AI is built on five foundational principles** 
- beneficence 
- non-maleficence
- autonomy 
- justice
- explicability

**Trustworthy AI should meet seven key requirements**
- human agency and oversight
- robustness and safety
- privacy and data governance
- transparency
- diversity and fairness
- societal and environmental well-being 
- accountability

### Transparency and Explainability

- AI and ML models should be transparent and explainable to improve trust and understanding among users. Explainable AI (XAI) and interpretable machine learning (IML) are crucial for making AI systems' decision-making processes clear and understandable.
- Provenance documentation can enhance transparency by providing a traceable record of data and model lineage, which helps in explaining complex models

### Robustness and Security:

- AI systems must be robust and secure to prevent manipulation, bias, and attacks. Ensuring the integrity of input data and the reliability of model outputs is critical for trustworthy AI. Robustness is the ability of a system to maintain its level of performance under any circumstances.  For AI systems, Robustness is often used to describe the ability of a model to maintain its level of performance under any circumstances, including external interference or harsh environmental conditions encompassing resilience and reliability and potentially more attributes, as related to proper operation of a system as it was intended by its developers. [[ISO/IEC DIS 22989]](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/74296/c4efbadbf1a146d4af6d62fcad09438f/ISO-IEC-22989-2022.pdf)

- Mechanisms for protecting AI inputs and guaranteeing proper outputs are necessary to maintain trust in increasingly complex AI systems. 

### Fairness and Unbiased Outcomes:

- AI models should be designed to produce fair and unbiased outcomes, avoiding discrimination against underrepresented groups. This includes auditing and accounting for biases in data and models

- Fairness is a key goal in AI system communication, ensuring that the models' decisions are equitable and just


### Privacy Preservation:

- Protecting user privacy is a fundamental aspect of trustworthy AI. AI systems should be designed to safeguard personal data and ensure that privacy is maintained throughout the AI lifecycle


### Accountability and Governance:

- AI systems should be accountable, with clear guidelines and frameworks for monitoring and governing their development, deployment, and use. This includes continuous monitoring and governance to ensure ongoing trustworthiness

- Providing assurance through verifiable credentials and a bill of materials (BOM) for data and models can help maintain accountability and scrutability


### Beneficence and Non-maleficence:

- Trustworthy AI should adhere to ethical principles such as beneficence (doing good) and non-maleficence (avoiding harm). These principles ensure that AI systems contribute positively to society and do not cause harm


### Autonomy and Justice:

- AI systems should respect user autonomy, allowing individuals to make informed decisions based on AI recommendations. Additionally, justice should be a guiding principle, ensuring that AI systems are fair and equitable
